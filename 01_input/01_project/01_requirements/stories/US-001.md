# US-001 Register and sign in

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

## Story

As a person who manages contacts, I want to register and sign in with my e-mail and password or with Google, so that I can reach my groups securely.

## Purpose

Every group and contact belongs to signed-in people, and invitations match on a verified e-mail address. Without a sign-in nobody can be told apart and the isolation of groups is impossible.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified e-mail | An e-mail address that Firebase marks as verified (clicking the link in Firebase's verification e-mail, or a Google account). |
| Empty state | The screen a user without any group sees. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-001-01 | The system shall offer sign-in with e-mail and password and "Sign in with Google" through Firebase, and no other provider and no multi-factor authentication. | Q-20, Q-32 |
| R-001-02 | When a person registers with e-mail and password, the system shall require a name of 1 to 100 characters. [ASSUMPTION] Google sign-in uses the Google account name; the name is needed for the History. | Q-25a |
| R-001-03 | If the password has fewer than 8 characters, then the system shall refuse the registration and show the reason. | Q-20 |
| R-001-04 | When a person has registered, the system shall have Firebase send its verification e-mail. | Q-20, Q-30a |
| R-001-05 | While the e-mail of the signed-in user is not verified, the system shall show only a "verify your e-mail" screen (with "send again" and "sign out") and shall open no group and no contact (BR-03). | Q-20 |
| R-001-06 | When a person requests a password reset, the system shall start Firebase's password-reset e-mail flow. | Q-20 |
| R-001-07 | If the e-mail or password is wrong, then the system shall show an error message and open nothing. | Q-20 |
| R-001-08 | While a Firebase session exists, the web app shall keep the user signed in across page reloads; when the user chooses "Sign out", the web app shall end the session and show the sign-in screen. | Q-20 |
| R-001-09 | When a user with a verified e-mail who is a member of no group signs in, the system shall show the empty state with one button, "Create a group". | Q-20 |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-001-01 | No account exists for `ana.novak@example.com` | The person registers with name "Ana Novak", that e-mail and password `Geslo1234` | An account is created and the "verify your e-mail" screen appears; Firebase has issued a verification e-mail; no group is shown | R-001-02, R-001-04, R-001-05 |
| AC-001-02 | Ana is signed in with an unverified e-mail | She opens the groups list or any group address | The "verify your e-mail" screen appears; no group and no contact data is shown | R-001-05 |
| AC-001-03 | Ana's e-mail is verified and she is a member of no group | She signs in with e-mail and password | The empty state with the button "Create a group" appears | R-001-09 |
| AC-001-04 | A Google account with a verified e-mail | The person chooses "Sign in with Google" | The person is signed in and, with no group, sees the empty state | R-001-01, R-001-09 |
| AC-001-05 | An account with password `Geslo1234` | The person signs in with password `Napacno123` | An error message appears; the person stays on the sign-in screen | R-001-07 |
| AC-001-06 | The person is on the sign-in screen | They choose "Forgot password" for `ana.novak@example.com` | Firebase's reset e-mail flow starts | R-001-06 |
| AC-001-07 | The person is on the registration form | They enter password `Geslo12` (7 characters) | The registration is refused with the reason; no account is created | R-001-03 |
| AC-001-08 | Ana is signed in | She reloads the page, then chooses "Sign out" | After the reload she is still signed in; after "Sign out" the sign-in screen appears | R-001-08 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-001-01 | Password of exactly 8 characters (`Geslo123`) | Accepted | R-001-03 |
| EX-001-02 | Password of 7 characters | Refused | R-001-03 |
| EX-001-03 | Name empty, or 101 characters | Registration refused; no account | R-001-02 |
| EX-001-04 | Name of exactly 100 characters | Accepted | R-001-02 |
| EX-001-05 | Registration with an e-mail that already has an account | Firebase's refusal is shown; nothing changes | R-001-01 |
| EX-001-06 | Unverified user presses "send again" | Firebase sends the verification e-mail again | R-001-04, R-001-05 |
| EX-001-07 | User verifies the e-mail in another tab and then reloads | The verified user continues to the groups list or the empty state | R-001-05, R-001-09 |

## Out of scope

Multi-factor authentication; providers other than e-mail/password and Google; e-mails of the product itself (only Firebase's e-mails exist); changing the e-mail address or the name after registration; account deletion (US-016).
