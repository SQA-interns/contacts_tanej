# US-016 Delete my own account

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

## Story

As a user, I want to delete my account, so that my personal data does not stay in the system when I no longer use it.

## Purpose

Users have a right to leave. The groups they worked in must keep working and keep accountability for past changes. Priority and MVP confirmed by the developer: the interview asks for the rule and journey J7 (a MVP journey) uses it, but gives no priority.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Account deletion | Removal of the user's Firebase account and of the user's memberships and e-mail address in the product. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-016-01 | When a signed-in user confirms the deletion of their account and is not the only Owner of any group, the system shall delete the account and remove all the user's memberships. | Q-20b |
| R-016-02 | If the user is the only Owner of one or more groups, then the system shall refuse, name those groups and ask the user to hand them to another member or delete them. | Q-20b |
| R-016-03 | When an account is deleted, the system shall keep the user's name in the History entries the user caused and shall remove the user's e-mail address from them. | Q-20b |
| R-016-04 | When an account is deleted, the system shall keep the contacts the user created. | Q-30 |
| R-016-05 | When an account is deleted, the system shall remove the user from the member lists and cancel nothing else of the groups. | Q-20b |
| R-016-06 | When an account is deleted, the system shall end the user's session; the user's next request is refused (401). | Q-12 |
| R-016-07 | The system shall ask for confirmation before deleting an account and shall require a recent sign-in. Firebase asks for a recent sign-in (confirmed by the developer). | Q-20b |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-016-01 | Marko is an Editor in "Konferenca 2026" and has changed Ana's phone | He confirms the deletion of his account | The account is deleted; the group shows no member Marko; the History entry still says "Marko Pirc" without `marko@example.com`; Ana's contact is unchanged | R-016-01, R-016-03, R-016-04, R-016-05 |
| AC-016-02 | Ana is the only Owner of "Konferenca 2026" | She asks to delete her account | Refused; the group is named and she is asked to hand over or delete it | R-016-02 |
| AC-016-03 | Ana made Marko Owner | She deletes her account | The account is deleted; Marko is the Owner | R-016-01, R-016-02 |
| AC-016-04 | Marko's account was deleted | An old token of Marko is used | The request is refused (401) | R-016-06 |
| AC-016-05 | The user is on the confirmation dialog | The user cancels | Nothing is deleted | R-016-07 |
| AC-016-06 | Boris is not signed in recently | He asks to delete his account | He is asked to sign in again first | R-016-07 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-016-01 | User is the only Owner of a group in Trash | Counts as sole Owner of that group: refused until it is handed over or erased | R-016-02 |
| EX-016-02 | User is a Viewer only | Allowed | R-016-01 |
| EX-016-03 | A pending invitation for the deleted e-mail address | The invitation stays; a new account with that address could accept it | R-016-05 |
| EX-016-04 | The user has no group | Allowed | R-016-01 |
| EX-016-05 | The user is the sole Owner of two groups | Both are named in the refusal | R-016-02 |

## Out of scope

Anonymising the name in the History (the name stays); deleting contacts the user created; export of the user's data; reactivating a deleted account.
