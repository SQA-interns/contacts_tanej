# US-003 Invite people to a group

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

## Story

As an Owner, I want to invite people to my group by e-mail address and role, so that my team can work on the same contacts.

## Purpose

A group is shared. Without invitations the Owner would work alone and could not give colleagues limited rights (for example read-only).

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending invitation | An invitation that is not accepted, not cancelled and not expired. |
| Accepted | The invited person signs in with the verified invited e-mail; there is no Accept or Decline step. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-003-01 | When an Owner enters an e-mail address and a role (Owner, Editor or Viewer) for the group, the system shall create a pending invitation. Any of the three roles may be offered in an invitation (confirmed by the developer). | Q-02c, Q-06 |
| R-003-02 | If the e-mail address has no valid format (BR-05), then the system shall refuse and create nothing. | Q-08b |
| R-003-03 | If the e-mail address (compared ignoring case, BR-07) belongs to a member of the group or to a pending invitation of the group, then the system shall refuse with a message and change nothing. | Q-30 |
| R-003-04 | If the group already has 20 pending invitations, then the system shall refuse a new one with a message. | Q-20a |
| R-003-05 | When a user signs in with a verified e-mail equal (ignoring case) to the address of a pending invitation, the system shall make the user a member with the invited role and show the group in the groups list, without any Accept or Decline step. | Q-02c, Q-20a |
| R-003-06 | The system shall treat an invitation as expired 14 days after its creation; an expired invitation gives no membership and does not count towards the 20 pending. | Q-20a |
| R-003-07 | When an Owner cancels a pending invitation, the system shall remove it, so that the invited person no longer sees the group. | Q-20a |
| R-003-08 | While the user is an Owner, the system shall list the group's pending invitations with e-mail address, role and expiry date. Needed to cancel (confirmed by the developer). | Q-20a |
| R-003-09 | The system shall send no e-mail of its own about invitations; the Owner informs the invited person. | Q-30a |
| R-003-10 | If the signed-in user's e-mail is not verified, then the system shall not match any invitation to the user. | Q-20 |
| R-003-11 | If a user who is not an Owner of the group tries to invite or cancel, then the system shall refuse (BR-02). | Q-06 |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-003-01 | Ana owns "Konferenca 2026" | She invites `marko@example.com` as Editor and `petra@example.com` as Viewer | Two pending invitations are listed with role and expiry date; no e-mail is sent by the system | R-003-01, R-003-08, R-003-09 |
| AC-003-02 | The invitation for `marko@example.com` is pending; Marko has registered and verified | Marko signs in | "Konferenca 2026" is in his groups list with the role Editor | R-003-05 |
| AC-003-03 | `marko@example.com` is already a member | Ana invites `Marko@Example.com` | A message says he is already a member; no invitation is created | R-003-03 |
| AC-003-04 | An invitation for `petra@example.com` is pending | Ana invites `petra@example.com` again | A message says an invitation is pending; nothing changes | R-003-03 |
| AC-003-05 | The group has 20 pending invitations | Ana invites a 21st address | A message says the limit of 20 is reached; no invitation is created | R-003-04 |
| AC-003-06 | An invitation for `petra@example.com` was created 14 days and 1 minute ago | Petra signs in with her verified e-mail | The group is not in her list; the invitation does not count towards the 20 | R-003-06 |
| AC-003-07 | An invitation for `petra@example.com` is pending | Ana cancels it; Petra then signs in | The invitation is gone; Petra sees no group | R-003-07 |
| AC-003-08 | An invitation for `ivan@example.com` is pending; Ivan registered but has not verified his e-mail | Ivan signs in | He sees the "verify your e-mail" screen only; the group appears after he verifies and signs in again | R-003-10 |
| AC-003-09 | Marko is an Editor | He tries to invite someone | The request is refused (403); no invitation is created | R-003-11 |
| AC-003-10 | Ana enters `marko@@example` | She submits | The e-mail is refused as invalid; nothing is created | R-003-02 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-003-01 | 19 pending, then one more invitation | Accepted (the 20th) | R-003-04 |
| EX-003-02 | 20 pending, one expires, then a new invitation | Accepted: the expired one does not count | R-003-04, R-003-06 |
| EX-003-03 | Signed in 13 days 23 hours after creation | Membership granted | R-003-05, R-003-06 |
| EX-003-04 | Signed in 14 days after creation, exactly or later | No membership | R-003-06 |
| EX-003-05 | Invited address has no account yet | The invitation stays pending; after registration and verification within 14 days the group appears | R-003-05 |
| EX-003-06 | A member removed earlier (US-004) is invited again | Accepted as a new invitation | R-003-03 |
| EX-003-07 | Same person invited to two groups | Both appear after sign-in | R-003-05 |
| EX-003-08 | Owner invites own e-mail address | Refused: already a member | R-003-03 |

## Out of scope

Invitation links; Accept or Decline; sending e-mails; changing the role of a pending invitation (cancel and invite again); limit on members; bulk invitation.
