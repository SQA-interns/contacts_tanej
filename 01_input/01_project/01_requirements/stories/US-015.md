# US-015 Delete and restore a group

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

## Story

As an Owner, I want to delete a group and be able to restore it for 30 days, so that I can retire a finished project without risking a mistake.

## Purpose

A finished project should disappear, but deleting a whole contact list by accident would be a disaster.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Group in Trash | A deleted group, kept for 30 days, hidden from all members' lists. |
| Deleted groups | The list, visible to Owners only, of groups in Trash where the user is an Owner. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-015-01 | When an Owner deletes a group and types the exact group name to confirm, the system shall move the group to Trash. | Q-06f |
| R-015-02 | If the typed name differs from the group name (case-sensitive), then the system shall not delete the group. [ASSUMPTION] case-sensitive. | Q-06f |
| R-015-03 | While a group is in Trash, the system shall hide it and its contacts from every member's groups list and answer "not found" to every request of a member who is not an Owner of it. | Q-06f |
| R-015-04 | While a group is in Trash, the system shall list it in "Deleted groups" for its Owners with the days left (BR-04). | Q-06f |
| R-015-05 | When an Owner restores a group within 30 days, the system shall return it with all members, contacts, tags, attributes, History and invitations as they were. | Q-06f |
| R-015-06 | When a group has been in Trash for 30 days, the system shall delete it permanently with its contacts, versions, History, memberships and invitations (BR-04). | Q-06f |
| R-015-07 | If a user who is not an Owner asks to delete or restore a group, then the system shall refuse (BR-02). | Q-06f |
| R-015-08 | While a group is in Trash, the system shall refuse every change to its contacts, members and invitations. | Q-06f |
| R-015-09 | When a group is deleted or restored, the system shall write a group-level History entry (US-013). | Q-25 |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-015-01 | Ana owns "Konferenca 2026"; Marko is an Editor | Ana deletes it and types `Konferenca 2026` | The group is gone from Marko's list and Ana's list; it appears in Ana's "Deleted groups" | R-015-01, R-015-03, R-015-04 |
| AC-015-02 | As above | Ana types `konferenca 2026` | Nothing is deleted | R-015-02 |
| AC-015-03 | The group is in Trash | Marko opens its address | "Not found" | R-015-03 |
| AC-015-04 | The group is in Trash | Ana restores it | It is back for all members with contacts, roles, History | R-015-05 |
| AC-015-05 | The group has been in Trash 30 days | The cleanup runs | It and all its data are gone; it is not in "Deleted groups" | R-015-06 |
| AC-015-06 | Marko is an Editor | He sends a delete request | Refused (403) | R-015-07 |
| AC-015-07 | The group is in Trash | An Owner tries to add a contact | Refused | R-015-08 |
| AC-015-08 | Two Owners, Ana and Boris; Ana deletes the group | Boris looks at his lists | He sees it in "Deleted groups" and can restore it | R-015-04, R-015-05 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-015-01 | 29 days 23 hours in Trash | Restorable | R-015-05 |
| EX-015-02 | Exactly 30 days | Erased by the next cleanup | R-015-06 |
| EX-015-03 | Pending invitation, then the group is deleted and restored | The invitation is still pending until its own expiry | R-015-05 |
| EX-015-04 | The name typed with surrounding spaces | Spaces are trimmed before comparing | R-015-02 |
| EX-015-05 | Two Owners delete the group at the same time | One succeeds; the other is told it is in Trash | R-015-01 |
| EX-015-06 | A contact in the group's Trash when the group is deleted | The contact's own 30 days continue unless the group is purged first | R-015-06 |

## Out of scope

Deleting the account (US-016); renaming a group; partial restore; a changeable retention period.
