# US-012 Delete to Trash, restore and delete forever

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

## Story

As an Owner or Editor, I want a deleted contact to go to a Trash first and to restore it, and as an Owner I want to erase it for good, so that nothing is lost by mistake and personal data can still be removed.

## Purpose

An accidental delete must be reversible, but personal data must not stay forever. Without Trash a mistaken click loses a contact; without a deadline erased people's data stays.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trash | The state of a deleted contact; the Trash view lists contacts in this state with the days left. |
| Delete forever | Immediate permanent erasure of a contact from the Trash by an Owner. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-012-01 | When an Owner or Editor deletes a contact, the system shall move it to Trash, remove it from the list, search, filters, export and duplicate search, and keep its data. | Q-06c |
| R-012-02 | While the user is an Owner or Editor, the system shall show a Trash view listing the group's contacts in Trash with the days left (BR-04). [ASSUMPTION] Viewers do not see Trash. | Q-06c, Q-23b |
| R-012-03 | When an Owner or Editor restores a contact from Trash, the system shall return it to the list with all its data (BR-04). | Q-06d |
| R-012-04 | When an Owner confirms "Delete forever" for a contact in Trash, the system shall erase it at once (BR-06). | Q-06d |
| R-012-05 | If an Editor or Viewer asks to "Delete forever", then the system shall refuse (BR-02). | Q-06d |
| R-012-06 | When a contact has been in Trash for 30 days, the system shall erase it permanently with its personal data (BR-04, BR-06). | Q-06c |
| R-012-07 | While a contact is in Trash, the system shall refuse edits to it (US-005 R-005-10). | Q-06c |
| R-012-08 | While the user is a Viewer, the system shall show no delete control and shall refuse delete and restore requests (BR-02). | Q-06 |
| R-012-09 | When a contact is deleted, restored or erased, the system shall write a History entry (US-013). | Q-25 |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| AC-012-03 | "Ana Novak" is in Trash | Ana (Owner) confirms "Delete forever" | The contact and its personal data are gone at once; it is not in Trash | R-012-04 |

| AC-012-05 | "Ana Novak" has been in Trash 29 days | The Editor restores it | It is restored | R-012-03 |
| AC-012-06 | "Ana Novak" has been in Trash 30 days | The system runs its periodic cleanup | The contact is gone with its personal data; no restore is possible | R-012-06 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-012-01 | Contact in Trash for 29 days 23 hours | Still restorable | R-012-03 |
| EX-012-02 | Contact in Trash for exactly 30 days | Erased by the next cleanup; no longer restorable once the cleanup ran | R-012-06 |
| EX-012-03 | Two Editors delete the same contact at the same time | One succeeds; the other is told it is already in Trash | R-012-01 |
| EX-012-04 | Restore of a contact that was already erased | "Not found" | R-012-03 |
| EX-012-05 | A contact is deleted and restored | The 30 days restart at the next deletion | R-012-01 |
| EX-012-06 | Delete a contact that is not in this group (by id) | "Not found" | R-012-01 |
| EX-012-07 | After erasure, its History entries | Remain, with contact data replaced (BR-06) | R-012-04, R-012-06 |
| EX-012-08 | Erased contact's tag was the only user of the tag | The tag is no longer offered | R-012-04 |

## Out of scope

Trash for groups (US-015); restoring several contacts at once; emptying all of the Trash at once; a changeable retention period.
