# US-004 Manage members and roles

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

## Story

As an Owner, I want to change roles, remove members and hand ownership to someone else, and as a member I want to leave a group, so that the right people have the right access at all times.

## Purpose

People change projects. Without role changes and removal, a former colleague keeps access to personal data; without a last-Owner rule a group could end up with nobody who can manage it.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Last Owner | The only member with the role Owner in a group. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-004-01 | While the user is a member of the group (any role), the system shall list the members of the group with name, e-mail address and role. Only Owners get the controls to invite, remove members or change roles (R-004-08). Developer answer. | Q-06 |
| R-004-02 | When an Owner changes a member's role to Owner, Editor or Viewer, the system shall apply the change at the member's next request; a promotion to Owner is never refused because of a group name (R-002-03). | Q-06a, Q-30 |
| R-004-03 | The system shall allow a group to have several Owners. | Q-06a |
| R-004-04 | When an Owner removes a member, the system shall delete the membership, keep the member's contacts and History entries (with the member's name), and remove the group from the member's list. | Q-30 |
| R-004-05 | When a removed member next requests the group or anything in it, the system shall answer "not found". | Q-30 |
| R-004-06 | When a member leaves a group, the system shall treat it like a removal of that member. | Q-30 |
| R-004-07 | If a removal, a role change or a leaving would leave the group without an Owner, then the system shall refuse it with a message and change nothing; this holds also when two such requests arrive at the same time. | Q-06a |
| R-004-08 | If a user who is not an Owner tries to change a role or remove another member, then the system shall refuse (BR-02). | Q-06 |
| R-004-09 | The system shall give no one the means to see or manage a group of which they are not a member (no system administrator). | Q-06a |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-004-01 | Ana (Owner) and Marko (Editor) are members | Ana changes Marko's role to Viewer | At Marko's next request he has no edit controls and edit requests are refused (403) | R-004-02 |
| AC-004-02 | Marko is an Editor | Ana removes Marko | Marko's next request for the group is answered "not found"; the group disappears from his list; his contacts and History entries remain with his name | R-004-04, R-004-05 |
| AC-004-03 | Ana is the only Owner | She tries to leave, or to demote herself to Editor, or to remove herself | Each is refused with a message; Ana is still the Owner | R-004-07 |
| AC-004-04 | Ana (Owner) and Marko (Editor) | Ana makes Marko Owner, then leaves the group | Marko is the Owner; Ana is no longer a member and her next request is answered "not found" | R-004-02, R-004-03, R-004-06 |
| AC-004-05 | Petra is a Viewer | She leaves the group | The group is no longer in her list | R-004-06 |
| AC-004-06 | Two Owners, Ana and Boris | Both demote themselves to Editor at the same time | At least one request is refused; the group still has an Owner | R-004-07 |
| AC-004-07 | Marko is an Editor | He tries to change Petra's role | Refused (403); nothing changes | R-004-08 |
| AC-004-08 | Boris is not a member of Ana's group | He asks for the member list by address | "Not found" | R-004-09 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-004-01 | Owner removes another Owner while at least one other Owner remains | Allowed | R-004-03, R-004-04 |
| EX-004-02 | Owner changes a member to the role the member already has | Nothing changes; no error | R-004-02 |
| EX-004-03 | Member was removed while the member's browser still shows the group | The next action is answered "not found" and the screen shows the group list | R-004-05 |
| EX-004-04 | A removed member signs in again and no invitation exists | The group is not shown | R-004-04 |
| EX-004-05 | Last Owner who deletes the account | See US-016 | R-004-07 |

## Out of scope

Transferring ownership by a separate action (an Owner makes someone Owner by changing the role); blocking a person from being re-invited; a limit on members.
